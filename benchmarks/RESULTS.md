## What these results cannot say

- Not field data. C-MAPSS trajectories come from NASA's C-MAPSS engine model; a positive or negative result here is about C-MAPSS degradation.
- No physics feedback. Replacing one engine does not change another in the replay; in a plant where maintenance changes the load on other equipment, the covariate-shift argument for VW would not hold as it stands.
- One policy family. The bound rule is tested at one threshold h; other policies select other checkpoints.
- Small strata. FD003 contributes 16 calibration units, and the shifted condition calibrates on 80 units and evaluates on 182.
- V1's statement covers a checkpoint drawn uniformly from a unit's life; it does not cover checkpoints a policy chose, nor NASA's truncation points. VW's statement is approximate because its policy depends on the calibration data through the fixed-point steps.
